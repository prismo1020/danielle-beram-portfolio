About

I’ve worked the floor, so I know what a useful answer looks like.

I lead global L&D at Sandbox VR. I built the learning function for 90 corporate and franchise locations in the US and abroad. My job is to turn a new process or product update into training people can use during a real shift.

Before this role, I spent seven years in multi-site retail leadership. I finished as a district manager responsible for 28 locations. That experience still shapes my work. If a course can’t help someone on a busy day, it isn’t finished.
