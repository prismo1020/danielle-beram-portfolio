About

I make complex operations teachable—and build the people and systems that keep them teachable at scale.

I lead global learning and development at Sandbox VR, where I established the learning function serving 90 corporate and franchise locations across the US and internationally. I partner across operations, product, support, and field leadership to turn business priorities into consistent execution for thousands of learners in five languages.

My work spans strategy and delivery: redesigning new-store readiness, developing 12 regional trainers and training leaders, certifying eight local training stores, governing a 300-plus-resource knowledge ecosystem, and building the content infrastructure behind it. Before Sandbox VR, I spent seven years in multi-site retail leadership, ending as a district manager over 28 locations. That operator background keeps my learning strategy grounded in what teams actually need on the floor.
