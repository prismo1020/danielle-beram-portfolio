Onboarding redesign

The first 90 days across 37 locations

A new hire could complete orientation, begin role training, and still depend on a manager to explain what came next or where to find an answer. The redesign connected orientation, role-based learning, manager sign-offs, the internal AI knowledge assistant, and 30-, 60-, and 90-day follow-up.

Before the redesign

Training existed, but the learner still had to navigate the gaps between it.

Orientation, role learning, manager coaching, and operational answers were handled separately. Completion did not always happen on time, and readiness depended heavily on the local manager.

New employees could remember that a topic had been covered without knowing where to find the answer when the question appeared during live work.

Learner journey

The first 90 days

01

Day one: orientation

Revised orientation introduced the guest journey and the operating expectations that applied across roles.

02

Before certification: role learning

Each learner received the modules, practice, and standards tied to the work they would perform.

03

Certification: manager sign-offs

Managers recorded observed skills against defined criteria instead of relying on a general confidence judgment.

04

First 30 days: answers during live work

Apollo, the internal AI knowledge assistant, let new hires retrieve approved guidance without knowing how the source library was organized.

05

Days 30, 60, and 90: reinforcement

Scheduled practice, validation, and retained-knowledge checks continued after orientation.

Manager workflow

What changed for managers

Managers received the role plan, the required LMS assignments, and the criteria for each observed sign-off. Completion was recorded against specific skills rather than a general judgment that the learner seemed ready.

The 30-, 60-, and 90-day checks also gave managers scheduled points to revisit performance after the initial orientation period.

Evaluation limits

What the data does and does not show

  • Results compare the six months before launch with the six months after launch across 412 hires at 37 corporate locations.
  • The intervention changed several connected elements at once, so the outcomes are attributed to the onboarding system rather than to one course or tool alone.
  • Manager-time figures are estimates based on observed question volume and retrieval time.
  • A separate annualized business-impact model exists, but modeled dollar value is intentionally excluded from the headline claims on this page.
